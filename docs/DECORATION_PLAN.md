# Decoration Plan

This document defines the reserved decoration zones for the next phase of the gym portfolio.

## Zone A: Cardio Wall

- Approximate location: left wall, front-left to mid-left sector near the treadmill.
- Asset category: hydration and wall-support props.
- Priority: high.
- Expected size: small to medium.
- Visual function: make the cardio wall feel serviced and lived-in without blocking the treadmill.
- Circulation constraints: keep a clear route behind and beside the treadmill.
- Recommended style: matte dark plastics, brushed metal, limited orange accents, cool-neutral tones.
- Approximate max count: 4 assets.
- Candidate asset types: bottles, towels, clock, wall sign, dispenser.

## Zone B: Strength Area

- Approximate location: rear-center zone around the dumbbell stand, arc bench, and mirrors.
- Asset category: free-weight and support props.
- Priority: high.
- Expected size: small to medium.
- Visual function: reinforce the strength station as a believable training area.
- Circulation constraints: preserve bench access and the central aisle.
- Recommended style: graphite, dark steel, muted yellow details, restrained warm accents.
- Approximate max count: 5 assets.
- Candidate asset types: discs, bar, rack shelf, kettlebells, towel support, secondary bench.

## Zone C: Functional Area

- Approximate location: right-side open floor around the mat and guided equipment.
- Asset category: mobility and conditioning props.
- Priority: high.
- Expected size: small to medium.
- Visual function: prepare the right side for a flexible training setup without crowding it.
- Circulation constraints: keep the area open enough for future interaction and traversal.
- Recommended style: dark rubber, charcoal plastics, warm-neutral textiles, white or cool lighting accents.
- Approximate max count: 6 assets.
- Candidate asset types: plyo box, balls, rope, foam roller, bands, kettlebells.

## Zone D: Entry Or Lateral Support

- Approximate location: front perimeter and lateral edge areas away from the main project machines.
- Asset category: reception and ambient support props.
- Priority: medium.
- Expected size: medium.
- Visual function: add hospitality and realism to the scene edges without stealing focus from the stations.
- Circulation constraints: avoid narrowing the entrance framing or blocking the camera composition.
- Recommended style: matte black, steel, muted wood or copper accent, minimal branding.
- Approximate max count: 3 assets.
- Candidate asset types: plant, small bench, water dispenser, narrow locker, bin.
